# PokéMaritimes: region plan (DRAFT v0.1)

> Status: Claude's first draft for Tom to edit. Nothing here is final.
> Purpose: decide the shape and progression of the region so maps get built
> once, not redrawn. Story detail comes later.

## Pillars
- Classic GBA feel (Emerald tileset language), Gen 1–6 Pokédex.
- The journey follows real Maritime geography, compressed and stylized.
- Each province feels different: **NB** forests, rivers, and the Fundy tides. **PEI** red sand and farmland. **NS** rocky Atlantic coast, a port city, and the highlands.

## Region at a glance (west → east)

| Area | Real inspiration | Feel / biome | Gating idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HomeTown** (start) | Small upper-Saint-John-River village | Rural, farms, forest edge | — |

| Acadian coast | Caraquet, Acadian Peninsula | Colourful fishing villages | Surf |
| **PEI** | Confederation Bridge, Charlottetown, north shore | Red cliffs, dunes, farmland, lighthouses | The bridge (or a ferry) |

| Sable Island | Wild horses, remote dunes | Isolated island | Post-game |

## Build order (sections)
1. **Section 1, upper Saint John River:** HomeTown → Route 201 → Woodstock → Route 202 → Fredericton. *These maps already exist.* This is the first playable slice.
2. Section 2: down the river to Saint John and the Fundy coast.
3. Section 3: Moncton → the bridge to PEI.
4. Then Nova Scotia, the highlands, and post-game.
